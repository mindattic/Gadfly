# Accommodations

Gadfly exams are spoken, on camera, and timed. If any of that doesn't work for you because of a disability or
another good reason, your instructor can adjust it for you. Ask **before** the exam: accommodations are set up per
student and exam by staff, never switched on by you in the exam room.

## What can be adjusted

| Accommodation | What changes | Who sets it |
|---|---|---|
| **Extra time** | Your time limit is multiplied (1.25×, 1.5× or 2×). The pause the examiner waits for before it takes your answer as finished gets longer too. Applies from your next attempt. | Your instructor or an admin |
| **Eye contact not expected** | No eye-contact setup and no reminders: the room doesn't dim and the examiner doesn't remind you about looking at the screen. It still notices if your face is out of view or someone else is there. Applies from your next answer. | Your instructor or an admin |
| **Leaving the exam window** | Normally, leaving the exam window three times or once for a minute marks your attempt for your instructor to look at. This raises that (for example 6 absences or 5 minutes, or 10 or 15 minutes) or turns the mark off. The exam still pauses while you are away and absences are still noted. For assistive technology that takes the focus, or a documented need to step away. See [Leaving the exam window](leaving-the-exam-window.md). | Your instructor |
| **Typed answers** | You type each answer instead of speaking it. The examiner still speaks the questions, your camera stays on, and the eye-contact check still applies unless that accommodation is also granted. Applies the next time you open the exam. | Your instructor or an admin |

Typed answers are meant for a documented speech, hearing or anxiety need. Typed answers are marked as typed for the
people who review your exam, and pasting into the answer box is noted for them to look at; nothing is blocked. **My
exams** shows which way you answer each exam: **Spoken exam, on camera**, or **Spoken questions, typed answers (your
accommodation)**.

Every grant and withdrawal is recorded in the audit log with who made it and when. Integrity notes from a student with
an eye-contact accommodation are kept at the lowest level and labelled as such for reviewers; a student with extra time
is never flagged by the check for answers that sound read out.

## What you can do yourself during the exam

These aren't accommodations, but they help many students:

- **Repeat the question** as often as you need, or press **Say it another way** to hear it reworded. Neither costs you anything.
- **Choose how long the examiner waits** before your silence sends the answer: standard, longer, much longer, or only when you
  press **Done answering**. Your choice is kept on this device.
- **Hold-to-talk**: in a noisy room, switch to holding a button while you speak (with the mouse, or by holding Space or
  Enter), so background noise isn't taken as your answer.
- **Pausing to think** is fine: the answer only ends after a pause, and starting to talk again cancels the
  countdown.
- **Clear and answer again** throws away what you have said so far for this answer, before it is sent. Once an answer
  is sent it is taken as heard; a reviewer sees which words the recognizer was unsure of.
- If your device asks for **reduced motion**, the examiner stays live but moves her head more gently; her lips still move
  with her words.
- **Taking the exam in a language that isn't your best?** Tell us on the get-ready page which language you speak best. The
  examiner then speaks a little more slowly and plainly and waits longer for you to finish. It is never used to grade you. See
  [Getting ready: the get-ready page and your languages](get-ready-and-languages.md).

## For instructors and admins: setting an accommodation

- **In the assessment**: open it, go to **6. Assign** (the exam's assignments list).
  - **Extra time**: choose it under **Extra time (accommodation)** when you assign the exam. It applies to the
    students you assign in that batch; to give extra time to one student, assign them on their own.
  - **Typed answers**: press **Grant typed answers** in the student's **Answers** column; **Withdraw** takes it back.
  - **Eye contact**: press **Declare accommodation** in the student's **Eye contact** column; **Withdraw** takes it
    back.
  - **Leaving the window**: choose a setting in the student's **Leaving the window** column: standard (3 absences or 1
    min), raised (6 absences or 5 min), raised (10 absences or 15 min) or never marks for review. You are asked to confirm;
    it applies from the next absence. This one is set on the exam's Assignments page only, not on the Assignments console.
- **On the Assignments console** (`/admin/assignments`, owners and admins): select one or more assignments and press
  **Accommodations** to set extra time, eye contact and typed answers for all of them at once. The **Accommodation**
  filter finds who has one. See [Assignments console](admin-assignments.md).

You don't need to record the reason in Gadfly. The [Instructor guide](INSTRUCTOR_GUIDE.md) has the details.

# Accommodations

Gadfly exams are spoken, on camera, and timed. If any of that doesn't work for you because of a disability or
another good reason, your instructor can adjust it for you. Ask **before** the exam: accommodations are set up per
student by your instructor, not switched on by you in the exam room.

## What can be adjusted today

| Accommodation | What changes | Who sets it |
|---|---|---|
| **Extra time** | Your time limit is multiplied (1.25×, 1.5× or 2×). The pause the examiner waits for before it takes your answer as finished gets longer too. | Your instructor, when assigning the exam to you |
| **Eye contact not expected** | The screen doesn't fade and the examiner doesn't ask you to look at it. It still notices if your face is out of view. Applies from your next answer. | Your instructor, on the exam's assignments list |

Both are recorded in the audit log with who set them and when. Integrity notes from a student with an eye-contact
accommodation are kept at the lowest level and labelled as such for reviewers; a student with extra time is never
flagged by the check for answers that sound read out.

## What you can do yourself during the exam

These aren't accommodations, but they help many students:

- **Repeat the question** as often as you need.
- **Hold-to-talk**: in a noisy room, switch to holding a button while you speak, so background noise isn't taken as
  your answer.
- **Pausing to think** is fine: the answer only ends after a pause, and starting to talk again cancels the
  countdown.
- **Correcting the transcript**: after every answer you see what was heard and can fix mis-heard words, or say the
  answer again.
- If your device asks for **reduced motion**, the examiner is shown as a still picture instead of a moving head.

## For instructors: setting an accommodation

1. Open the assessment and go to **6. Assign** (or the exam's assignments list).
2. **Extra time**: choose it under **Extra time (accommodation)** when you assign the exam to the student.
   It applies to the students you assign in that batch; to give extra time to one student, assign them on their own.
3. **Eye contact**: in the **Assignments** table, press **Declare accommodation** in the student's **Eye contact**
   column. **Withdraw** takes it back. You don't need to record the reason in Gadfly.

The [Instructor guide](INSTRUCTOR_GUIDE.md) has the details.

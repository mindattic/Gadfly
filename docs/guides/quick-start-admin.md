# Quick start for admins and owners

Setting up an organization and running a first exam. The [Admin guide](ADMIN_GUIDE.md) has the detail.

1. **Sign in.** MindAttic creates your organization and gives you the owner's user name and a temporary password.
   At first sign-in you choose a new password and set up an authenticator app (every staff role uses one).
2. **Check the settings.** **Admin > Settings**: how long what students said is kept (90 days by default), whether
   your organization is in the EU or EEA (voice measurements are then off by default), whether instructors see only
   what they created, a monthly cost ceiling, and whether one person may grade alone.
3. **Invite your staff.** **Members > Invite by email**, or **Invite many (CSV)** with one `email,role` per line. Roles:
   Owner, Admin, Instructor, Examiner, SecondReviewer, Student. Each person gets a link valid for 72 hours.
4. **Invite your students** the same way (role Student), or add them on the admin desk's **Students** tab with a
   user name and password.
5. **Create and assign an exam.** Either an instructor builds an assessment from source material (see the
   [instructor quick start](quick-start-instructor.md)), or you use the admin desk's **Exams** tab: paste or upload
   notes, **Create exam**, then **Build objectives & publish**, and assign it on **Student exams**.
6. **Try it as a student.** Add yourself a test student, sign in as them in a private window, and take the exam.
7. **Watch it through to a result.** Grading, approval and publishing happen under **Grading**.

## Good to know

- **Mock mode.** A "MOCK MODE" banner across the top means no AI provider is in use: questions and scores are
  simulated and must not be used to grade anyone.
- **Separation of duties.** The person who scored a grade can't approve it. In a one-person organization, turn on
  **Allow a single grader**; each use is recorded in the audit log.
- **Privacy requests.** **Members** has **Export data** and **Erase data…** for each person. Erasure can't be undone.
- The admin pages are being extended in this release (dashboard, students, exams, assignments, live sessions,
  audit log, system status). Their help topics are under **Running your organization**.

# Before your exam: a checklist

Ten minutes of preparation prevents almost every problem on the day. Tick the boxes as you go (the ticks are only
for you; nothing is saved).

## The day before

- [ ] I can sign in. (Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page now, not five minutes before the exam.)
- [ ] I can see the exam under **My exams**, and I know when its window opens and closes.
- [ ] I know how long the exam is and how many attempts I have.
- [ ] I have practised explaining each topic **out loud**, in my own words, including *why* things are true.
- [ ] I have taken the [practice interview](practice-interview.md) (**Meet your examiner** on **My exams**) once, on the device and in the room I'll use. Nothing from it is kept or counted.
- [ ] If I need an accommodation (extra time, or not being held to eye contact), I have asked my instructor and it is set up. See [Accommodations](accommodations.md).

## Your equipment

- [ ] I'm using a computer or tablet with a **microphone and camera**. A headset with a microphone is best: it cuts background noise and stops the examiner's voice being picked up as yours.
- [ ] I'm using a current version of **Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari**. See [browser problems](troubleshooting-browser.md) if you're not sure.
- [ ] My device is charged or plugged in.
- [ ] I have closed other apps that might use the microphone or camera: Zoom, Teams, Meet, FaceTime, Discord, OBS, and other browser tabs with calls.
- [ ] Notifications are off or on "do not disturb", so nothing pops up or talks over the examiner.

## Your room

- [ ] It's quiet, and I won't be interrupted for the length of the exam (tell the people you live with).
- [ ] There's light **in front of me**, not behind me, so the camera can see my face.
- [ ] My camera is at about eye level and my face fits in the picture.
- [ ] The screen is where I'll naturally look at the examiner (the eye-contact check calibrates on where the examiner is on your screen).

## Your connection

- [ ] I'm on a steady connection. If Wi-Fi is weak, I'll sit near the router or use a cable.
- [ ] Nobody else in the house is streaming video or downloading large files during the exam.
- [ ] I'm not using a VPN that slows the connection or blocks live connections, unless my organization requires one.

## Fifteen minutes before

- [ ] Open **My exams** and press **Start** on the exam. Nothing is recorded and no attempt is used until you press **Begin exam**.
- [ ] Allow the microphone and camera when the browser asks.
- [ ] Every system check shows ✓ (a **!** warning is fine). If something shows ✗, fix it and press **Check again**: [microphone](troubleshooting-microphone.md), [camera](troubleshooting-camera.md), [browser](troubleshooting-browser.md).
- [ ] Try the practice question out loud, so you hear yourself answer once.
- [ ] Read the notice. When you're ready, tick the box and press **Begin exam**.

## During the exam, remember

- Speak in your own words. Pausing to think is fine: start talking again and the answer continues.
- Look at the examiner while you answer. Looking away to think is normal; if you look away from the screen for a long while, the examiner tilts her head, then the space around her dims slightly (it clears as soon as you look back), then at most once she says a warm line at a pause.
- "I don't know" is an honest answer, and a better one than a guess dressed up.
- Each answer is taken as heard: there is no read-back or do-over, so finish your thought before the countdown sends it.
- If the exam pauses, fix what it says and press **Try again**. The clock stops for up to 10 minutes in all.
- Don't close the page until you see **Your answers were submitted**.

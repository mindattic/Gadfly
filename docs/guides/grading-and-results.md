# How grading and results work

**People decide every Gadfly grade.** An AI panel gives a preliminary view, but a human examiner grades
independently, a disagreement is reconciled, and an authorized person approves before you see anything. Here is
each step, and what you see at the end.

## The steps

1. **The exam ends.** Your answers are submitted, and your exam shows **Submitted** on **My exams**.
2. **AI preliminary grading.** A panel of several AI models scores each learning objective against your course
   material, on a scale of levels from 0 (insufficient evidence) to 5 (transfer and critical reasoning), with
   reasons and the parts of your answers it relied on. This is a starting point, not a grade.
3. **Human grading.** An examiner (or your instructor) scores each objective from your transcript. By default they
   grade **blind**: they don't see the AI's view, or any integrity notes, until their own scores are in.
4. **Comparison.** Gadfly puts the AI and human scores side by side and combines them using the weights your
   instructor set before the exam (by default 60% human, 40% AI). If the AI found no usable evidence for an
   objective, the human score stands alone for it.
5. **Reconciliation, when needed.** If the two differ by more than the set threshold, or the score sits right on a
   grade boundary, a reviewer looks at both, records why they differed and resolves it.
6. **Approval.** An authorized person (an instructor, admin or owner, never the person who entered the human score)
   approves the final grade, or sends it back for re-grading.
7. **Publication.** Only now is the result released to you.

Integrity notes are **never part of the grade**, at any step.

## What you see

When your result is published, **My exams** shows **Your result** with your grade as a percentage (and a label, if
your organization uses one), and **See the assessment report**. The report shows:

- each learning objective, its weight, and the AI, human and combined scores;
- the examiner's comment, and what was missing from your answers;
- the parts of your answers used as evidence;
- what the next level asks for, and, after an earlier attempt, how each objective changed since;
- knowledge gaps, the approval, and any appeal.

**Download the report (Markdown)** or **Download the report (PDF)** saves a copy, and **Download your completion
certificate (PDF)** a one-page certificate. The result page also offers your official **transcript**, and lists any
**integrity notes** on the attempt with their plain explanation (never part of the grade; you can respond to them in an
appeal). See [Transcript downloads](transcript-downloads.md). If a result is later amended, it is marked "amended".

## How long it takes

It depends on your instructors: grading is done by people. Your result appears on **My exams** as soon as it is
published; nothing is shown before then, not even the AI's preliminary scores.

## If you think the result is wrong

You can appeal a published result. See [Appeals](appeals.md).

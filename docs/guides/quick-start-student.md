# Quick start for students

Five steps from your invitation to a submitted exam.

1. **Sign in.** Use the link in your invitation email to choose a password, or the user name and temporary
   password your instructor gave you (you'll choose a new one at first sign-in).
2. **Open My exams.** Your assigned exams are listed with their window and attempts. You aren't emailed when an
   exam is assigned; check here.
3. **Press Start and get ready.** Allow your microphone and camera, say a few words so the meter moves, and wait
   until every check shows ✓. Try the practice question out loud. Read the notice, tick the box, press **Begin exam**.
   (Nothing is recorded and no attempt is used until you press Begin exam.)
4. **Talk with the examiner.** It asks one question at a time, out loud. Answer out loud in your own words while
   looking at the examiner. Stop talking and your answer is sent after a short pause, taken as heard: there is no
   read-back or do-over, so say what you mean before it goes. "I don't know" is an honest answer.
5. **Wait for your result.** People grade your answers. When the result is published, it appears on **My exams**
   with a full report. You can appeal it.

## Before the day

- Work through the [checklist](before-your-exam.md): headset, quiet room, light in front of you, other call apps
  closed.
- Practise explaining each topic **out loud**, including the *why*.
- Need extra time, or can't keep eye contact? Ask your instructor for an [accommodation](accommodations.md) now.

## If something goes wrong

- The get-ready page shows ✗: [microphone](troubleshooting-microphone.md), [camera](troubleshooting-camera.md),
  [browser and connection](troubleshooting-browser.md).
- The exam pauses: fix what it says and press **Try again**. The clock stops for up to 10 minutes in all.
- The page freezes: reload it, then **Resume** from **My exams**. Your answers so far are saved.

The [Student guide](STUDENT_GUIDE.md) has everything in detail.

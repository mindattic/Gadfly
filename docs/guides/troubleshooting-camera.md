# Camera problems

Every Gadfly exam needs your camera on: you keep eye contact with the examiner as in a real conversation. **The
picture is never recorded or sent**; it is analysed on your own device. This page helps when the camera won't work.

## What the get-ready page is telling you

| The Camera check says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **waiting for your browser** | The browser is asking for permission. | Choose **Allow** in the prompt near the address bar. |
| **not allowed** | The camera is blocked for this site. | Allow it for this site (below), then press **Check again**. |
| **none found** | The browser can't see a camera. | Connect a webcam, open a laptop's lid fully, or turn the camera on in your system settings. |
| **in use by another app** | Another program has the camera. | Close Zoom, Teams, Meet, FaceTime, OBS, camera apps and other tabs with calls, then press **Check again**. |
| **shows no picture** | The camera opened but sent no picture within a few seconds. | Check it isn't covered (many laptops have a privacy shutter or a camera key), that no other app has it, then press **Check again**. |
| **stopped working** | It worked, then disappeared. | Plug it back in, then press **Check again**. |
| **this browser can't use it** | The browser doesn't support the camera here. | Use a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on an `https://` address. |

## Quick fixes

1. **Open the privacy shutter** or press the laptop's camera key (often F8, F10 or a key with a camera symbol).
2. **Allow the camera for this site** in the browser (the same place as the microphone; see below).
3. **Close every other app and tab that might use the camera.** Only one app can usually use a camera at a time.
4. **Pick the right camera** if you have several (built-in, external webcam, a virtual camera from OBS or a
   meeting app). In Chrome: `chrome://settings/content/camera`; in Edge: `edge://settings/content/camera`.
5. **Restart the browser**, and if that doesn't help, the computer.

## Allow the camera

The steps are the same as for the microphone, with **Camera** in place of **Microphone**:

- **Chrome, Edge**: the padlock or sliders icon left of the address bar, then **Camera: Allow**.
- **Firefox**: the camera icon in the address bar, or the padlock, then remove **Blocked**; reload and allow.
- **Safari (Mac)**: **Safari > Settings for This Website > Camera: Allow**.
- **iPhone and iPad**: **aA > Website Settings > Camera: Allow**, or **Settings > Apps > Safari > Camera**.
- **Chrome on Android**: site settings icon > **Permissions** > **Camera**; and in the phone's **Settings > Apps >
  Chrome > Permissions > Camera**.

And in the operating system:

- **Windows 11 and 10**: **Settings > Privacy & security > Camera**: turn on **Camera access**, **Let apps access
  your camera** and **Let desktop apps access your camera**.
- **macOS**: **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera**: turn on your browser, then quit and reopen it.
- **ChromeOS**: **Settings > Privacy and security > Privacy controls > Camera access**.

[Microphone problems](troubleshooting-microphone.md) has the browser steps in more detail.

## The camera works, but the eye-contact check struggles

- **Light in front of you**, not behind. A window behind you makes your face a silhouette.
- **Camera at eye level**, your whole face in the picture, about an arm's length away.
- **Keep the examiner where you look.** If you moved the window or your chair, press **Set up eye contact again**.
- A message that the eye-contact check couldn't start means your device lacks a feature it needs; the exam carries
  on without it.

See [Eye contact and the screen fade](eye-contact-and-the-screen-fade.md).

## If the camera stops during the exam

The exam pauses and says what happened ("The camera wasn't allowed…", "Your camera stopped working…"). Fix it and
press **Try again**; the examiner repeats the question. The clock stops while the exam is paused, for up to
10 minutes in all.

## Still stuck?

Try another browser or device, test the camera in your system's camera app, and tell your instructor before the
exam window closes, with your browser, your device and what the Camera check says.

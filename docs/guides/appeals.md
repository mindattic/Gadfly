# Appeals

If you believe a published result is wrong, you can ask for it to be reviewed.

## How to appeal

1. Open **My exams** and press **See the assessment report** under your result.
2. On that page (**Your result**), write in **Your reason** why you think the grade is wrong (up to 4,000 characters).
3. If the page lists **Integrity notes on this attempt**, you can also write in **Your response to the integrity
   notes** what happened at those moments. It is optional, and you can send it on its own, without a reason. It
   reaches your instructor inside the appeal, under the heading "About the integrity notes on this attempt:". The
   reason and response together can be up to 4,000 characters.
4. Press **Submit appeal**.

You see "Your appeal was submitted. Your instructor will review it; your published result stays visible
meanwhile." **My exams** then shows "appeal under review" next to your grade.

## Writing a good appeal

- Point to the **objective** you think was graded wrongly (the report lists each one).
- Say **what in your answers** shows the understanding you think was missed. The report quotes the parts used as
  evidence; you can quote others from your transcript (**Download my data** has the full transcript).
- If a word was mis-heard in a way that changed the meaning, say so.
- Keep it factual. Reviewers read it next to your answers.

## What happens next

An instructor reviews the appeal and either:

- **confirms the grade**, or **dismisses** the appeal, and your result stays as it was; or
- **amends the grade**, with a reason. Your result is then marked "amended" and the new grade is shown.

You see the outcome on the report page. You can have one open appeal on a result at a time.

## If you're still not satisfied

Gadfly records the appeal and its outcome, but your institution's own rules decide what happens beyond that. Ask
your instructor or your institution's office for academic appeals.

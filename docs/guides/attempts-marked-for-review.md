# Attempts marked for instructor review

Gadfly never cancels an exam, deletes answers or fails anyone automatically. When something needs a person's judgment, the
attempt is marked **Needs instructor review** instead, and its result is held until a person decides. This page says why an
attempt is marked, where you see it, and how to decide.

## Why an attempt is marked

| Reason | When | What to look at |
|---|---|---|
| **Left the exam window** | The student left the exam window three or more times, or once for a minute or more (a [window-absence accommodation](accommodations.md) can raise this or turn it off). | Each absence (time, length, during which question), the student's optional explanation, and the answers given around those times. |
| **Korean attempt: AI scores not yet calibrated** | Every attempt taken in a language other than English, until a bilingual calibration shows the AI panel agrees with human graders on that language. | The AI objective grades marked cross-lingual and uncalibrated: check them against the student's own answers (and a labelled machine translation if you need one). |

What happens in the exam room when a student leaves the window: the exam pauses, the examiner stops and the answer waits with
its words kept; on return a neutral line says the exam paused and the examiner repeats the end of the question. When an attempt is
marked, the student is told at once that a person will look at it, nothing is cancelled, and they may leave an explanation that
only staff see. Focus can be lost for innocent reasons (notifications, system dialogs, screen readers and other assistive
technology), and no study has validated window-focus events as evidence of cheating, so the mark is a reason to look, never a
finding.

## Where you see it

- **Review list** and **grading queue**: a **Needs instructor review** badge with a short reason; both lists can be filtered to
  marked attempts.
- **Admin desk, student exams**: the same badge.
- **Review page** and **grading page**: a panel with the reason and when it was marked, each absence from the exam window (time,
  length, question), the student's explanation (or "The student left no explanation"), the language reason, and how many AI
  objective grades are cross-lingual and uncalibrated.

Under blind grading, the absences and the student's explanation are hidden from a grader until their own blind score is in.

## While it is marked, the result is held

A marked attempt is treated as unpublished everywhere: **Publish** is refused, and the student's result, transcript copy,
certificate and the grade sent back to an LMS wait until a person decides.

## Deciding

Staff who may approve final grades (instructors, admins and owners) decide, in one of two ways:

- **Clear the mark** on the review or grading page, with a required note saying what you looked at and why the attempt stands; or
- **Approve the final grade with a note** in Grading.

Both are recorded in the audit log with your note, and the panel then shows who decided, how and when. If the attempt does not
stand, deal with it as you would any other concern: talk to the student, reset the attempt or follow your institution's
process. Gadfly does not decide that for you.

# Microphone problems

Every Gadfly exam is spoken, so the microphone has to work before **Begin exam** turns on. This page goes from the
quickest fixes to the less common ones. Most problems are a permission that was refused once, or another app that
is holding the microphone.

## What the get-ready page is telling you

| The Microphone check says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **say a few words out loud** | The microphone is open but hasn't heard you yet. | Speak normally for a couple of seconds and watch the meter. If it doesn't move, click anywhere on the page and speak again. |
| **waiting for your browser** | The browser is asking for permission. | Look for the permission prompt near the address bar and choose **Allow**. |
| **not allowed** | The microphone was blocked for this site, by you or by a setting. | Allow it for this site (below), then press **Check again**. |
| **none found** | The browser can't see any microphone. | Plug in a headset or microphone, or turn one on in your system settings (below). |
| **in use by another app** | Another program has the microphone. | Close Zoom, Teams, Meet, Discord, FaceTime, OBS and other tabs with calls, then press **Check again**. |
| **stopped working** | It worked, then disappeared (unplugged, a Bluetooth headset that went to sleep). | Plug it back in or reconnect it, then press **Check again**. |
| **this browser can't use it** | The browser doesn't support the microphone here. | Use a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, and make sure the address starts with `https://`. |
| **couldn't be opened** | Something else went wrong. | Try the steps below, then **Check again**. Restarting the browser fixes most of these. |

**Check again** also re-opens a microphone that failed, so you don't need to reload the page.

## Quick fixes that solve most problems

1. **Allow the microphone for this site** in the browser (see your browser below).
2. **Close other apps that use the microphone**, including other browser tabs.
3. **Check the right microphone is chosen** in the browser's site settings. Laptops often have several
   (built-in, headset, webcam microphone, a virtual one from a meeting app).
4. **Unmute.** Many headsets have a mute switch on the cable or the ear cup; some laptops have a microphone key.
5. **Restart the browser** (close every window of it, then open it again).

## Allow the microphone in your browser

### Chrome (Windows, Mac, Chromebook, Linux)

1. Click the **site settings icon** at the left of the address bar (it looks like a padlock or two sliders).
2. Set **Microphone** to **Allow**. If you don't see it, choose **Site settings** and set it there.
3. To choose which microphone: open `chrome://settings/content/microphone` and pick it from the list at the top.
4. Reload the page if Chrome asks, then press **Check again**.

### Edge (Windows, Mac)

1. Click the **padlock** at the left of the address bar, then **Permissions for this site**.
2. Set **Microphone** to **Allow**.
3. To choose which microphone: open `edge://settings/content/microphone`.

### Firefox (Windows, Mac, Linux)

1. Click the **microphone or permissions icon** in the address bar (if it is crossed out, the microphone is
   blocked) and remove the block, or click the padlock and clear **Use the microphone: Blocked**.
2. Reload the page; when Firefox asks, choose the right microphone from the list and press **Allow**.

### Safari (Mac)

1. In the menu bar choose **Safari > Settings for This Website** (or **Settings > Websites > Microphone**).
2. Set **Microphone** to **Allow**.

### Safari and other browsers on iPhone and iPad

1. Tap **aA** (or the page menu) in the address bar, then **Website Settings**, and set **Microphone** to **Allow**.
2. Or open the **Settings** app, choose **Apps > Safari** (or **Safari**), then **Microphone**, and choose
   **Allow** or **Ask**.
3. Make sure no other app (a call, a voice memo) is using the microphone.

### Chrome on Android

1. Tap the **site settings icon** left of the address, then **Permissions**, and allow **Microphone**.
2. If Chrome itself isn't allowed: open the phone's **Settings > Apps > Chrome > Permissions > Microphone** and
   allow it.

## Allow the microphone in your operating system

If the browser is allowed but still hears nothing, the system may be blocking the browser.

### Windows 11 and 10

1. Open **Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone** (Windows 10: **Settings > Privacy > Microphone**).
2. Turn on **Microphone access** and **Let apps access your microphone**, and **Let desktop apps access your
   microphone** (browsers count as desktop apps).
3. Choose the right microphone in **Settings > System > Sound > Input**, and check the input level moves when you
   speak.

### macOS

1. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone**.
2. Turn on your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). macOS asks you to quit and reopen it.
3. Choose the right microphone in **System Settings > Sound > Input**, and check the input level moves when you
   speak.

### ChromeOS (Chromebook)

1. Open **Settings > Privacy and security > Privacy controls**, and turn on **Microphone access**.
2. Choose the right microphone in **Settings > Device > Audio > Input**.

## The examiner doesn't seem to hear me during the exam

- **Watch "What I heard".** If words appear there, the examiner is hearing you; the transcript is what it heard.
- **A noisy room?** Press **Switch to hold-to-talk** and hold **Hold to talk** while you speak.
- **The examiner's voice is being picked up**: use headphones or a headset, or turn the speaker volume down.
- **A Bluetooth headset** can switch to a low-quality "headset" mode or fall asleep. A wired headset is more
  reliable. If it disconnects, the exam pauses: reconnect it and press **Try again**.
- **Mis-heard words** aren't a microphone fault. Answers are taken as heard (there is no read-back); the words the
  recognizer was unsure of are marked for the person who reviews your exam. Speaking clearly, close to the microphone,
  in a quiet room helps most.

If the microphone stops during the exam, the exam pauses and says what happened. Fix it and press **Try again**.
The clock stops while the exam is paused, for up to 10 minutes in all.

## Still stuck?

- Try another browser from the list, or another device.
- Test the microphone in another app (the system's voice recorder) to see whether the problem is the device itself.
- Tell your instructor before the exam window closes. Say which browser and device you are using and what the
  Microphone check says.

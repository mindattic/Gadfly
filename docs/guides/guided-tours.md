# Guided tours

Each main page has a short guided tour. It points at the real parts of the page one at a time, with a few sentences on what
each one is for. A tour starts by itself the first time you open the page, and only once. You can see it again whenever you
like.

## Taking a tour

- **Next** and **Back** move between steps. The right and left arrow keys do the same.
- **Skip tour** or the **Esc** key closes the tour at any step. **Done** closes it at the end.
- A step that points at something not on the page right now (an empty list, a panel for another grading stage) is left out.
- While a tour is open, the rest of the page waits. When it closes, you are back where you were.

Tours work with a keyboard and a screen reader: the step is read out as it changes, and the keyboard stays inside the tour
until you close it. If your device is set to reduce motion, the tour moves between steps without any sliding.

## Seeing a tour again

- Press **Show me around** at the top of the page.
- Or use the links under **Replay a tour** at the end of this page. They list the tours for the pages your role can open;
  each link opens the page and starts its tour.

The tour of one grading session, one review and the get-ready page need a particular session or exam, so for those Help
tells you where to go, and you press **Show me around** once you are there.

## When a tour shows again

"Shown once" is remembered by your browser for your account. A tour can show again if you use another browser or device,
clear your browser's site data, or browse privately, and when a page changes enough that its tour is updated.

There is never a tour inside the exam room or the practice interview: nothing covers the examiner while you are answering.
The get-ready tour only points at the system check. It does not open, stop or test your camera or microphone.

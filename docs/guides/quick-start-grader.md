# Quick start for examiners and second reviewers

Your job is the human half of every grade. Five things to know, then the [Grading guide](grading-guide.md) has the
detail.

1. **Sign in with two-step verification.** Accept your invitation, choose a password, and set up an authenticator
   app at first sign-in.
2. **Open Grading.** The queue lists ended attempts and where each one stands. **Start grading** opens one.
3. **Examiners: score blind.** Read the transcript, then for every learning objective choose a **Level** (0 to 5) and
   **Score**, write a **Comment** the student will see, and note your **Supporting evidence**. Press **Submit
   evaluation for all objectives**. Until you do, the AI's view and all integrity notes stay hidden, on purpose.
4. **Second reviewers: reconcile.** When the AI and human scores differ a lot, the grade waits at **Reconciliation
   Required**. Record the **Cause**, any resolved scores and the **Resolution**, then **Record resolution and
   recalculate**. You can't reconcile a grade whose human score you entered yourself.
5. **Then it moves on.** An instructor, admin or owner approves and publishes. You'll see the status change in the
   queue.

## Good to know

- Grade the answers, not the student's delivery: accent, pace and nerves aren't understanding. The transcript shows
  the student's confirmed words and any corrections they made.
- Words the speech recognizer was unsure of, and the student's own corrections, are shown; give the student the
  benefit of a plausible mis-hearing.
- Integrity notes are never part of the grade. If you see one after submitting, read
  [Reading integrity notes](integrity-notes.md) before drawing any conclusion.

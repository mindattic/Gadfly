# Privacy in plain words: what is and is not recorded

This page says, without legal language, what Gadfly keeps about an exam, what never leaves your device, how long
things are kept and who can see them. The notice you agree to before each exam says the same for that exam,
including your organization's retention period.

## Never recorded

- **No video.** Your camera picture stays on your screen. The eye-contact check and the camera checks run on your
  own device; no image, frame or face measurement is sent.
- **No audio is kept.** Your voice is streamed to a speech-to-text service to turn it into words while you speak.
  The sound itself is not stored by Gadfly.
- **Not your screen.** Gadfly doesn't record or capture your screen.
- **No guess about your emotions or honesty.** Gadfly never labels how you feel or whether you are telling the truth.

## Recorded

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Every question, and the **transcript** of your spoken answers, with the corrections you made | So people can grade what you said |
| When each question was asked and when you started and finished answering | Timing is part of the record of the conversation |
| **How you spoke**: pace, pauses, hesitations, how sure the speech recognizer was, whether more than one voice was heard | Context for the reviewer, and so the examiner can ask a follow-up |
| **Voice measurements** (unless they are off for you; see below) | Context only; never part of the grade |
| **Eye-contact notes**: when you looked away while answering, for how long, whether the screen faded | Context for the reviewer; never part of the grade |
| **Browser notes**, if your exam has them: switching tab or window, copying the question, leaving full screen, a second screen | Context for the reviewer; nothing is blocked |
| **Camera notes** on some exams: face not in view, a second face, a possible change of person | Context for the reviewer |
| The AI panel's **preliminary judgment** of each answer, and the people's grades, comments and approvals | Your result |
| Your **consent**: the time, and a fingerprint of the exact notice you saw | Proof of what you were told |

Every integrity note (eye contact, browser, camera, voice, an answer that reads unlike your others) is a
**low-confidence note for a person to read in context**. None of them is ever a verdict, ever part of your grade,
or ever fails you on its own. They are hidden from an examiner grading blind until their grade is in.

## Voice measurements

During each spoken answer the browser measures a few things about your voice (pitch, its steadiness, loudness, how
much of the time you were speaking) and sends only those numbers, never the sound. They are compared with your own
first answers. You see a short line about it in your own transcript. Reviewers see the raw differences, always with
the reminder that nerves, effort, a hard question or the room change a voice. The comparison may make the examiner
ask a further question; it never changes a grade.

**They are off by default in the EU and EEA**: for organizations there, and for students marked as living there.
When they are off, the numbers are thrown away before anything is stored, and nothing about your voice steers the
questions.

## How long things are kept

- **What you said** (the transcript, word timings, voice measurements, the raw detail of integrity notes) is kept
  for your **organization's retention period**, 90 days unless your organization chose another (between 7 days and
  10 years). The notice before each exam says how long. After that it is removed automatically, and answers read
  "[redacted after the retention period]".
- **The outcome** (questions, scores, your grade, comments, the approval, the consent record) is kept with your
  exam until your organization deletes it.

## Who can see it

- **You** see your result and report once published, and can download everything held about you.
- **Your organization's staff**, by role: instructors see the exams they run (and, depending on a setting, only
  those they created); examiners and reviewers see what they grade; admins see the whole organization.
- **Nobody outside your organization** sees your exams. Each organization's data is kept separate.
- **AI providers** that judge answers receive the questions and your answers (not your name, account details, or
  any video). The speech service receives your voice while you speak, to make the transcript.

## Your choices

- **See it**: **Download my data** at the top of **My exams** gives you one file with everything held about you in
  that organization.
- **Delete it**: ask your organization's administrator. It removes your exam records in that organization and
  can't be undone.
- **Don't agree**: leave the get-ready page without ticking the box. Nothing is started and no attempt is used.
- **Leave early**: **Finish and leave the exam** at any time. What you answered is submitted and the attempt is used.

## Questions

Ask your organization's administrator. Your organization decides how long data is kept and who in it sees what.

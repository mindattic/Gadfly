# Page-turn sound effects

Two single page-turn/book-rustle variants, used interchangeably to avoid repetition fatigue.
Both are unmodified Freesound "hq" preview MP3s (the public preview file Freesound serves to
every visitor, logged in or not; same audio as the original upload, re-encoded by Freesound
for delivery).

# Typing ambience (GAD-T502)

`typing-1.mp3` and `typing-2.mp3` (20 s seamless loops, 64 kbps mono) replace the page turns under the examiner's
thinking pause. They are synthesized, not recorded: soft, muffled keystrokes across a small room over a faint noise bed,
generated by `tools/sounds/make_typing.py` (seeds 1005 and 2210) and encoded with ffmpeg/LAME. No third-party audio, so
no license applies beyond this repository's own.

# Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, 2000

## The World Cup and the Dark Mark

The book opens with a short scene at the Riddle house, where **Frank Bryce**, an old caretaker, overhears **Voldemort**, **Wormtail** and a plan to kill Harry, and is killed. Harry then goes with the Weasleys and Hermione to the **Quidditch World Cup**, where Ireland beats Bulgaria though the Bulgarian Seeker **Viktor Krum** catches the Snitch. That night masked Death Eaters terrorise Muggle campsite owners, and someone casts the **Dark Mark**, Voldemort's skull-and-snake sign, into the sky. It is the first sign in thirteen years that his followers are active.

**Winky**, the house-elf of **Barty Crouch**, is found holding Harry's wand under the Mark, and Crouch dismisses her in humiliation. Hermione's response, founding **S.P.E.W.**, the Society for the Promotion of Elfish Welfare, shows her anger at house-elf slavery and her inability to persuade others, including Ron, to take it seriously.

## The Triwizard Tournament

Hogwarts hosts the **Triwizard Tournament**, a revived contest between three schools: **Beauxbatons** (with its headmistress **Madame Maxime** and champion **Fleur Delacour**), **Durmstrang** (**Igor Karkaroff** and Krum) and Hogwarts. A magical **Goblet of Fire** chooses one champion per school, and an **Age Line** keeps under-17s out. Hogwarts' champion is **Cedric Diggory**, a decent Hufflepuff, but the Goblet then ejects a fourth name: **Harry Potter**. Harry insists he did not enter, but he is bound by a **binding magical contract**, and nearly everyone assumes he cheated for glory. Ron, jealous, stops speaking to him for weeks, and the press, led by the malicious reporter **Rita Skeeter**, distort his story.

The new Defence teacher, **Alastor "Mad-Eye" Moody**, is a famous Auror. He teaches the **Unforgivable Curses**, the Imperius, Cruciatus and Killing curses, and Neville is badly shaken to see the Cruciatus on a spider, since his parents were tortured with it. Moody also helps Harry, hinting what each task needs.

- **First task:** a dragon, a **Hungarian Horntail**, guards a golden egg. Harry summons his **Firebolt** with a **Summoning Charm** and flies around it.
- **Second task:** the egg's cry, a song decoded in the prefects' bath with Cedric's hint, leads to the lake, where Harry must rescue a hostage held by the **merpeople**. Dobby supplies **Gillyweed** so Harry can breathe underwater, and he refuses to leave any hostage behind.
- **Third task:** a hedge **maze** with magical obstacles, with the **Triwizard Cup** at its centre.

Between the tasks there is the **Yule Ball**, which brings awkward dances, Hermione's date with Krum and Ron's jealousy. The book's characters here are grown up enough to quarrel about feelings, not merely about school.

## The graveyard and Voldemort's return

Cedric and Harry reach the Cup together and agree to take it jointly, to share the honour. It is a **Portkey** and carries them to a graveyard in **Little Hangleton**. **Wormtail** appears, kills Cedric on Voldemort's order ("Kill the spare") and ties Harry to a gravestone. He then performs a dark ritual with a cauldron, using **bone of the father, flesh of the servant** (Wormtail's own hand) and **blood of the enemy**, taken by force from Harry. Voldemort is **restored to a body**, and the blood taken from Harry means he can now touch him without suffering Lily's protection. He summons the **Death Eaters**, who come in fear, mocks those who abandoned him and gives Harry a duel.

When the two wands meet, their cores, both from Fawkes the phoenix, connect in a golden thread, and **Priori Incantatem** makes Voldemort's wand display its recent victims as shadows. The echoes of Cedric, Harry's parents and others distract Voldemort and give Harry a chance to break the link, grab Cedric's body and Portkey back to Hogwarts.

## Who put Harry's name in the Goblet

Moody takes Harry away, and then he reveals himself. He is **Barty Crouch Junior**, the son of Barty Crouch senior and a Death Eater who was sent to Azkaban, from which his dying mother rescued him by swapping places with him. His father kept him hidden under the **Imperius Curse** until he broke free at the World Cup. Disguised through **Polyjuice Potion**, he has impersonated the real Moody, who is held in a trunk, for the whole year. His motive is fanatical loyalty to Voldemort. He put Harry's name in the Goblet with a **Confundus Charm** under another school's name, and manipulated the tasks to make sure Harry won and touched the Portkey. Dumbledore and Snape expose him with **Veritaserum**. Crouch senior was murdered by his son, and Fudge's Dementor performs the **Kiss** on Crouch junior before he can testify.

## Key characters and their motives

- **Voldemort** wants to be restored to a body and to destroy Harry, and his plan is long and patient, relying on servants who owe him devotion or fear him.
- **Barty Crouch Junior** is a fanatic who spent years confined by his own father. His devotion to Voldemort is the one thing that drives him, and he is tormented by what he sees as a debt to his master.
- **Cedric Diggory** is fair and honourable. He warns Harry about the dragons, though a rival, and his decency is what makes his death so shocking.
- **Karkaroff** is a former Death Eater who bought his freedom by naming others, and he fears what Voldemort's return means for him.
- **Rita Skeeter** exploits public gossip for sales and shows how the press can manufacture a reputation.
- **Ron and Hermione** are tested by jealousy and rivalry, and their quarrels show that their friendship can bend and mend.

## Consequences and themes

- **Politics and denial:** Fudge refuses to believe Voldemort is back, since it would disturb his position. Dumbledore warns that the Ministry's choice to deny the danger will cost lives, and begins to gather allies.
- **Loss and innocence:** Cedric's death is the first time a decent, unprepared peer dies. Dumbledore tells the school that Cedric was "a good and loyal friend" killed by Voldemort, and that the choice between what is right and what is easy must be faced.
- **Unity and division:** the tournament was meant to build bonds between schools. Dumbledore urges unity, warning that discord and mistrust help Voldemort.
- **Trust and deception:** the greatest threat was a trusted teacher.

Harry gives his thousand-galleon prize to Fred and George for their joke shop. Voldemort is back and strong, Karkaroff flees in fear since he had been a Death Eater, and Harry returns to the Dursleys with a war about to start.

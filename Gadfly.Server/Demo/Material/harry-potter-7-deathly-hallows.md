# Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows, 2007

## Flight and the fall of the Ministry

The final book takes place almost entirely outside Hogwarts. **Voldemort** has taken control of the **Ministry of Magic** and the wizarding world, installing a puppet minister, **Pius Thicknesse**, and creating a **Muggle-born Registration Commission** under **Dolores Umbridge**. This regime persecutes Muggle-borns as thieves of magic, which turns the blood-purity ideology of the earlier books into official policy. The book shows how fear and propaganda let such a system take hold.

The Order moves Harry from Privet Drive on his seventeenth birthday, when his protection will end, using **Polyjuice Potion** to create seven Harrys. The operation goes badly: Death Eaters attack, **Mad-Eye Moody** and **Hedwig** are killed, and George loses an ear. Soon after, **Rufus Scrimgeour** is killed by Voldemort, **Snape** becomes headmaster of Hogwarts, and the trio go into hiding. They are guided only by Dumbledore's cryptic bequests: the **Deluminator** to Ron, the book of **The Tales of Beedle the Bard** to Hermione, and the **Golden Snitch** to Harry. The **Sword of Gryffindor** is also left to Harry but the Ministry refuses to hand it over.

## The Horcrux hunt

The mission to find and destroy the **Horcruxes** is made hard by the lack of knowledge, because Dumbledore left very little. The first Horcrux they find is the **locket**. **Regulus Black**, the **R.A.B.** of the previous book, once stole it and died, and **Kreacher** the house-elf helps them find where it ended up, with Umbridge. They break into the Ministry, in disguise, to retrieve it. They cannot destroy it, and wearing it makes them quarrelsome and wretched as the soul fragment feeds on their fears. Ron leaves them in anger and jealousy. He returns after the **Deluminator** guides him back, rescues Harry from a frozen pool where a **doe Patronus** has led Harry to the real Sword of Gryffindor, and destroys the locket himself. This is the moral turning point for Ron.

Goblin-made weapons absorb what strengthens them, which is why the sword and **basilisk fang venom** can destroy Horcruxes. The **cup** is held in Bellatrix Lestrange's vault at **Gringotts**, and the trio rob the bank with the goblin **Griphook**, who betrays them. The **diadem** is in the Room of Requirement at Hogwarts, where it is destroyed by **Fiendfyre** cast by Crabbe, who dies. **Nagini** is the last one, apart from Harry.

## The Deathly Hallows

The title refers to a different quest. In **The Tale of the Three Brothers**, three brothers cheat Death and are given the **Elder Wand**, the **Resurrection Stone** and the **Cloak of Invisibility**. The older brothers die through the first two, while the third uses the Cloak and greets Death as an old friend. Hermione at first treats this as a children's tale, but the symbol on the story appears everywhere, including on Grindelwald's mark. **Xenophilius Lovegood** explains it.

Harry is torn between hunting Horcruxes and chasing the Hallows. **Voldemort** wants the **Elder Wand**, which he believes will make him invincible, and takes it from Dumbledore's tomb. Its ownership passes by defeating the previous master, not by possession: **Grindelwald** to **Dumbledore** to **Draco**, who disarmed Dumbledore, and then to **Harry**, who overpowers Draco at Malfoy Manor. Voldemort's mistake is to kill Snape, thinking Snape was the master. The Hallows contrast Voldemort's desire to avoid death with Harry's acceptance of it.

At **Godric's Hollow**, they find the grave of Harry's parents, and **Bathilda Bagshot**'s body is taken over by Nagini, who attacks. At **Malfoy Manor** they are captured; **Dobby** arrives to save them and dies at Shell Cottage, with Harry digging his grave by hand.

## Dumbledore, Snape and the truth

Harry learns that Dumbledore's past is more complicated than he thought. A scandalous biography by **Rita Skeeter** and the tale of **Ariana** told by Dumbledore's brother **Aberforth** reveal his youthful friendship with **Grindelwald** and his dream of a world ruled by wizards. Harry must decide whether to trust Dumbledore, and chooses to, seeing that Dumbledore changed and fought his own hunger for power.

Snape's memories, given up as he dies, reveal that he loved **Lily** all his life, switched sides when Voldemort targeted her, and acted as Dumbledore's agent. They also reveal that Dumbledore knew Harry was a Horcrux and must die, and that Snape was to kill Dumbledore as a mercy because the cursed ring had already doomed him. Snape is neither hero nor villain: his courage and love coexist with bullying and cruelty.

## The Battle of Hogwarts and the ending

Harry goes to the forest holding the **Resurrection Stone** from the snitch, which opens at the close, and is met by the shades of his parents, Sirius and Lupin. He lets Voldemort strike him. In a limbo that resembles King's Cross he meets Dumbledore, who explains that Harry's willing sacrifice destroyed the soul fragment in him and that Voldemort's blood-protection spell, which keeps Voldemort alive, was tied to Harry, so Harry can return. Lupin, Tonks, Fred and others die in the fighting. **Neville** beheads Nagini with the sword, and **Molly Weasley** kills Bellatrix.

In the final duel, Voldemort's Killing Curse rebounds because the Elder Wand will not kill its true master. Harry, who keeps the Cloak, mends his own wand and returns the Elder Wand to Dumbledore's tomb so that its power ends. The **epilogue**, nineteen years later, shows Harry and Ginny, Ron and Hermione at King's Cross, sending their children to Hogwarts.

## Key terms to know

- **Deathly Hallows:** the Elder Wand, the Resurrection Stone and the Cloak of Invisibility.
- **Elder Wand:** a wand whose allegiance passes by defeating its owner, not by killing.
- **Resurrection Stone:** the Hallow that summons shades, hidden in the Golden Snitch.
- **Muggle-born Registration Commission:** the Ministry body persecuting Muggle-borns.
- **Sword of Gryffindor:** a goblin-made sword that absorbs what makes it stronger, such as basilisk venom.
- **Fiendfyre:** cursed fire that can destroy Horcruxes but is nearly impossible to control.
- **Dobby and Kreacher:** house-elves whose loyalty and treatment make the book's point about respect.
- **Aberforth Dumbledore:** Albus's brother, who keeps the Hog's Head and the secret passage to Hogwarts.
- **Snape's memories:** the revelations of his love for Lily and his loyalty to Dumbledore.
- **Kings Cross limbo:** the place where Harry meets Dumbledore after being struck down.

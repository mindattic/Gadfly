# Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince, 2005

## A world at war and a new teacher

The sixth book begins with the wizarding world openly at war. Voldemort's return is no longer denied, **Fudge** has been replaced by **Rufus Scrimgeour**, and Death Eaters, Dementors and giants are causing disasters that are blamed on accidents. Dumbledore now treats Harry as a partner and gives him private lessons, which is a major change from the previous book, where he kept Harry at a distance.

Dumbledore persuades his former colleague **Horace Slughorn** to return as Potions master. Slughorn is a collector of gifted and well-connected students, flattering them for the favours they may later give him. He is also a man with a secret shame: as a young teacher he told **Tom Riddle** about **Horcruxes**. This makes him the key to the entire book, because Dumbledore needs the true memory of that conversation.

At the same time, **Harry** finds an old Potions textbook annotated by the **Half-Blood Prince**. Its improved instructions make Harry the best student in the class and win Slughorn's admiration. The book is attractive but dangerous: it contains a curse, **Sectumsempra**, whose effect Harry does not understand. He uses it on **Draco Malfoy** in a bathroom duel and nearly kills him. The episode shows that talent and cleverness without judgment and moral limits can be deadly, and it also shows Harry's unreflective trust in an unknown source.

## The memories of Tom Riddle

Dumbledore uses a **Pensieve**, a basin that holds memories, to show Harry Voldemort's past. The memories reveal that Riddle's mother, **Merope Gaunt**, from a degraded pure-blood family descended from Slytherin, used a **love potion** on a Muggle, **Tom Riddle Sr.**, then stopped giving it, and he abandoned her. She died after giving birth in an orphanage. The boy grew up unloved, learned he could control and frighten others, and at eleven met Dumbledore, who was suspicious of him from the start.

These memories explain Voldemort's motives. He hates his Muggle father, hides his weak origins, collects trophies from the people he hurt, and chooses objects tied to the founders of Hogwarts because he wants to make himself the heir of a great tradition. Slughorn's altered memory is finally restored when Harry, using the luck potion **Felix Felicis**, persuades Slughorn to give him the real one. In it, Riddle asks how a wizard might split his soul more than once, and Slughorn's answer shows that Voldemort probably made six Horcruxes, planning seven pieces of soul in all. Dumbledore explains that Harry became an accidental Horcrux when Voldemort's curse rebounded in the attack on the Potters, though he does not yet say so.

Dumbledore has already found and destroyed the **Gaunt ring**, and the cost was a cursed, blackened hand, held back from killing him only by **Snape**'s skill. Together they identify the **diary**, the **ring**, Slytherin's **locket**, Hufflepuff's **cup**, Nagini and possibly something of Ravenclaw's.

## The cave and the Astronomy Tower

**Draco Malfoy**, after his father's failure at the Ministry, has been ordered by Voldemort to kill Dumbledore, as punishment for Lucius and as a task he is expected to fail. Draco tries and botches several attempts, including a **cursed necklace** that hurts Katie Bell and a poisoned bottle of mead that nearly kills Ron. His real plan is a **Vanishing Cabinet** in the Room of Requirement, which he repairs to let Death Eaters into the school. His mother **Narcissa**, afraid for him, asks **Snape** to protect him, and Snape binds himself to this with an **Unbreakable Vow** to finish the task if Draco cannot. Harry is convinced that Snape and Draco are both dangerous, and he is partly right.

Dumbledore takes Harry to a sea cave where Voldemort once hid a Horcrux. Dumbledore drinks a draining potion in the basin, is attacked by **Inferi** and survives only because Harry helps him. They carry off the locket, which turns out to be a fake, with a note signed **R.A.B.**, who has taken the real one.

Back at Hogwarts, Death Eaters, including **Fenrir Greyback**, are inside the castle and the Dark Mark hangs over the Astronomy Tower. Dumbledore, weak and with Harry frozen under his Invisibility Cloak, faces Draco, who disarms him and then cannot do it. He is shaking and tearful. Dumbledore speaks kindly to him and offers protection, and other Death Eaters arrive. **Snape** kills Dumbledore with the **Killing Curse** at his own quiet request, and flees with Draco. Harry pursues him and Snape reveals that he is the Half-Blood Prince, the son of **Eileen Prince**.

## Love, loss and choosing the quest

Alongside the war, the book follows ordinary adolescent romance. **Ron** dates **Lavender Brown** to Hermione's unhappiness, **Harry** falls for **Ginny Weasley**, and Harry and Ginny begin a relationship. After Dumbledore's death Harry ends it, to protect her from Voldemort. These stories matter because they remind us what the war is fought for.

Dumbledore also tells Harry the central message of the book. Voldemort cannot understand the power that protects Harry, and Dumbledore insists that Harry's capacity for love is what makes him different, not any special magical skill. The prophecy matters only because Voldemort believed it, though now it is true because he made it so.

## The ending

Dumbledore's death leaves the school in grief and Harry in a state of loss. The funeral draws wizards, merpeople and centaurs from far and wide, and the Ministry's authority is shaken. Harry decides not to return to Hogwarts and instead to hunt the remaining Horcruxes, as Dumbledore asked him. **Ron** and **Hermione** insist on coming with him, and the book closes with Harry's decision to see the quest through, however long it takes.

## Key terms to know

- **Pensieve:** a basin in which memories can be stored and viewed.
- **Horcrux:** a hidden fragment of soul that anchors a wizard to life.
- **Half-Blood Prince:** the pseudonym of Snape, in whose old textbook Harry finds improved potions and spells.
- **Sectumsempra:** the slashing curse Harry uses on Draco without knowing its effect.
- **Felix Felicis:** liquid luck, used by Harry to win Slughorn's true memory.
- **Vanishing Cabinet:** the pair of cabinets Draco repairs to let Death Eaters in.
- **Unbreakable Vow:** the binding oath Snape makes with Narcissa.
- **Inferi:** reanimated corpses guarding the Horcrux in the cave.
- **R.A.B.:** the initials on the note that reveals the locket was stolen.
- **Slughorn's Slug Club:** his circle of favoured, well-connected students.
